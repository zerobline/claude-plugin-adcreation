## Ask for help or report a problem

Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/zerobline/claude-plugin-adcreation/issues) for bug reports, setup questions, inaccurate skill behavior, broken resources or feature suggestions.

When reporting a bug, include the plugin version, OpenAI product (ChatGPT or Codex), the skill being used, a concise reproducible prompt with **all private/client information removed**, what you expected, and what happened. Share screenshots only when you have rights to share them and have removed confidential data.

Support is provided on a best-effort basis. There is no promised response time or service-level agreement.

## Common questions

**Why can't I publish to the OpenAI directory?** Public submission availability is controlled by OpenAI. If your portal says it is not accepting new skills-only plugins, changing a field or making an empty MCP server cannot remove this restriction.

**Why did a design produce no file?** Skill instructions alone do not create file or image tools; the current ChatGPT/Codex product must support the requested artifact output.

**Is a 20+/25 score proof that the ad works?** No. It is a subjective creative heuristic. Validate it with real audiences.

**Can I report a privacy issue?** You may contact the maintainer via GitHub Issues for general privacy questions. **Do not post personal information, private campaign content or identity documents** publicly. Data handled inside ChatGPT and Codex is managed through OpenAI's policies and account controls.

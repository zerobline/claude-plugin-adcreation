# Step 7 — Taste Test and Iteration Loop

**Codename: The 5D Grid + A/B War Room**

This is where most "pretty good" ads stay stuck — not because they lack value, but because they **settle before they sting**. Sharpen the signal until it slices.

Purpose:

- Score the ad across five taste dimensions.
- Pressure-test multiple variants fast.
- Lock in only what earns emotional recognition and platform elegance.

The question: *"Does this ad hit in silence — without caption, CTA, or clever context?"* If not, cut deeper and sharpen harder.

## 7.1 The 5D Taste Calibration Grid — the Creative Scorecard

Rate 1–5 in each dimension:

| Dimension | Ask | If low, fix by… |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Is the core message instantly obvious? | Remove clever phrasing. Cut to the bone. |
| Surprise | Does it flip a cliché or subvert an expected tone? | Revisit the belief flip or the metaphor. |
| Emotion | Do I *feel* something — shame, joy, relief? | Push deeper into the contradiction. |
| Recognition | Would the tribe say "that's literally me"? | Adjust the voice or visual for resonance. |
| Visual Power | Would I stop scrolling (or look twice from the street) on the look alone? | Increase contrast. Strip filler. |

Score:

- **20–25** → Publish. Screenshot bait.
- **16–19** → Tweak one visual, one word, one tone.
- **Below 16** → Rebuild from the emotional Spike Core.

Bonus filter: *"Would someone share this with one word: 'Mood'?"*

## 7.2 Test variants rapidly — the A/B War Room

Don't pick a favourite. **Build the collision.**

The 2×2 Remix Grid:

| | Visual A | Visual B |
|---|---|---|
| **Copy A** | Punchline A + Format A | Punchline A + Format B |
| **Copy B** | Punchline B + Format A | Punchline B + Format B |

- **Punchline A** = the emotional spike winner.
- **Punchline B** = a sharper or softer remix.
- **Visual A** = the best scroll-stopper format.
- **Visual B** = a secondary native format.

Ask: Which one **stings faster**? Which is **most shareable**? Which triggers **DMs**, not just likes? Keep the one or two with the **fastest hit and cleanest silhouette**.

## 7.3 Harvest real feedback — the Reality Loop

Get cold eyes. Not compliments.

**1. Peer Provocation Test.** Ask someone from the tribe:

- "Would you share this or scroll past it?"
- "What's the first emotion this gave you?"
- "If this was made for you, would it feel like it *gets* you?"

**2. AI ad critique.** Hand the ad to a reviewer that hasn't seen how it was made, with the brief:

> "Act like a savage creative director. Tear this ad apart. Be honest, not polite. Rate clarity, emotional punch, visual power."

In this plugin, that's the `ad-critique` skill's cold review mode — run in a fresh-context subagent when the environment supports one.

**3. Micro-audience live test.** Post two or three variants in an Instagram Story poll, a WhatsApp or Discord group, or a Slack channel. Ask "Which one hits hardest?" Track **DMs, saves, and shares** — not likes.

For billboards and posters: show each variant for three seconds at thumbnail size, then ask what it said and how it made them feel. If they can't repeat the line, it won't survive the road.

If no one sends it to a friend → back to the Spike Core.

## The ad is ready when it's built on

- Emotional truth
- Logical rebellion
- A tribal mirror
- Visual clarity
- Platform-native taste

This is no longer content. It's creative psychology, engineered to resonate.

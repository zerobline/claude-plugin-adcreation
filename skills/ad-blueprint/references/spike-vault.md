# The Spike Vault

**Codename: The Emotional Swipe Locker** — "When you can't write from scratch, swipe something sharp."

These aren't taglines; they're emotional flashbangs. Use the vault to:

- generate spiky insights fast when blocked,
- give beginners proven lines to remix,
- calibrate scoring: every 5 awarded elsewhere should feel as exposed as the 🔴 lines here.

| Mark | Level | Feels like |
|---|---|---|
| 🔹 | Safe (1–2) | Mild empathy; could be a warm-up post |
| 🔸 | Stingy (3–4) | Reader nods, might pause |
| 🔴 | Diary-worthy (5) | Feels like a secret being exposed |

Copy these, then upgrade them. Never ship a vault line verbatim as the final ad; it's a starting point to be made specific to the actual tribe and product.

## Creators & coaches

**Shame lines**

- "They teach confidence, but still ask friends if their content is cringe." 🔴
- "They call themselves 'multi-passionate' — but secretly feel like quitters." 🔴
- "They want to help others grow, but feel like they're falling apart inside." 🔴

**Contradictions**

- "They crave freedom — but miss the structure they left." 🔸
- "They love building — but hate being seen starting over (again)." 🔴
- "They believe in clarity — but live in browser-tab chaos." 🔴

**Default beliefs to flip**

- "Success = more content." → ✖ Wrong: success = more resonance.
- "More offers = more income." → ✖ Only if they're irresistible.
- "If I'm not visible, I'm invisible." → ✖ No. Silence can be strategy.

## Solo founders & freelancers

**Shame lines**

- "They tell clients they're fully booked — but haven't hit income goals in 3 months." 🔴
- "They say they value freedom — but are terrified of hiring." 🔴
- "They post testimonials — but never mention the refund that broke them." 🔴

**Contradictions**

- "They love being independent — but wish someone else would decide for once." 🔸
- "They say they're chill — but check Stripe every 2 hours like it's a pulse monitor." 🔴

**Default beliefs to flip**

- "Just work harder." → ✖ You're not building — you're buffering.
- "Charge what you're worth." → ✖ Your worth isn't for sale. Your outcome is.

## B2B buyers & managers

**Shame lines**

- "They call it 'due diligence' — it's mostly fear of being the one who picked wrong." 🔴
- "They sign off on the safe vendor — then spend the year defending it in meetings." 🔴
- "They present the roadmap with confidence — and pray nobody asks about last quarter's." 🔸

**Contradictions**

- "They want innovation — but only the kind someone else has already tried." 🔴
- "They demand hard ROI proof — but bought the current tool on a good demo and a gut feeling." 🔸

**Default beliefs to flip**

- "Nobody gets fired for buying the market leader." → ✖ They just quietly fall behind.
- "More features = safer choice." → ✖ More features = more things nobody uses.

## Everyday consumers

**Shame lines**

- "They say they 'don't have time to cook' — then scroll recipes for 40 minutes." 🔴
- "They want to be the relaxed parent — but run the weekend like a military operation." 🔴

**Contradictions**

- "They crave a quiet weekend — but feel guilty the second they get one." 🔸
- "They buy the gym membership to feel in control — and avoid the app that tracks it." 🔸

**Default beliefs to flip**

- "Treat yourself = buy more." → ✖ Sometimes the treat is needing less.
- "Cheaper is smarter." → ✖ Cheaper twice isn't.

## Power prompt — remix the vault

Take one 🔴 line and reframe it into a punchline format:

- "You're not [X]. You're just [hidden truth]."
- "Not [assumption]. [New identity]."
- "Still [problem]? Try [mindset shift]."

Then visualise it as a screenshot, meme structure, or poster in Step 4.

## Empty templates for new categories

When the audience isn't covered above, build a mini-vault first (three lines each), score it, and keep only 🔴 lines as Spike Core candidates:

```
CATEGORY: [tribe]
Shame lines:     "They [public behaviour] — but [private feeling]."
Contradictions:  "They [want X] — but [hate/fear the cost of X]."
Beliefs to flip: "[Category cliché]." → ✖ [What's actually true].
```

# Step 3 — Write a Sticky Punchline

**Codename: The Punchline Arsenal**

The emotional truth and the belief flip are ammunition. Now load the gun.

Purpose:

- Forge one unskippable line that hits in under 3 seconds.
- Make the audience *feel* something before they *think*.
- Create screenshot-worthy lines that live in memory, not just feeds.

A good insight gets ignored if the line doesn't hit. This is not a tagline; it's a **moment of emotional interruption**. A punchline is short, specific, strangely true, and impossible to ignore. If it takes longer than 3 seconds to get, it's already dead.

## 3.1 Choose the emotional weapon — the Punchline Selector

Anchor the line on **one** emotion:

| Weapon | Effect | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Shame Mirror | Reflects a hidden fear | "You're not lazy. You're just scared." |
| Contradiction Cut | Twists expected logic | "Not structure. Permission." |
| Identity Unlock | Reveals a new self-image | "Messy minds. Powerful results." |

Pick the one that fits the Spike Core and the belief flip.

## 3.2 The 3-Second Truth Test — the Pressure Cooker

Write three versions with the proven templates:

1. **"Not [thing]. [Truth]."** → "Not a gym. A judgment-free zone."
2. **"You're not [X]. You're just [emotional truth]."** → "You're not unmotivated. You're overwhelmed and alone."
3. **"Still [problem]? Try [new mindset/action]."** → "Still stuck? Try finishing ugly."

- Say each out loud.
- If it doesn't land in a single breath → cut it.
- If you don't *feel* it → scrap it.

## 3.3 The Micro-Loop

Every punchline must pass three filters:

- **Speak it out loud** — is it smooth and sharp?
- **Test it on someone cold** — do they flinch, laugh, or nod?
- **Simplify a word** — does it hit harder when cleaner?

Final test: *"Would someone screenshot this line and send it to a struggling friend?"* If yes, lock it in. If no, simplify or sharpen until it earns that moment.

## Length budgets by placement

| Placement | Main line budget |
|---|---|
| Roadside billboard | 7 words or fewer |
| Street / transit poster | about 10 words, plus one small supporting line |
| Social static | one breath; supporting text goes in the caption |
| Print | headline in one breath, up to two short supporting lines |

## 25 punchline templates

Use these to generate candidates fast; the Spike Core supplies the content.

**Shame Mirror**

1. You're not [label]. You're just [hidden truth].
2. It's not [excuse]. It's [real reason].
3. You don't need [more X]. You need [less Y].
4. [Public behaviour]. [Private feeling].  — "Fully booked. Still terrified."
5. Everyone thinks you're [image]. You know you're [truth].
6. [Thing they say]. [Thing they mean].
7. Be honest: [uncomfortable question]?
8. [Number] [tabs/apps/plans]. Zero [outcome].

**Contradiction Cut**

9. Not [assumption]. [New truth].
10. Not [thing]. [Better thing].
11. [Old rule] is over. [New rule].
12. Stop [common advice]. Start [counter-move].
13. Less [X]. More [Y].
14. [Expected thing] is the problem.
15. The [category] that [does the opposite of category norm].
16. [Positive word] is overrated. [Underrated word] isn't.
17. Still [problem]? Try [new mindset/action].

**Identity Unlock**

18. [Hidden trait]. [Empowering truth].  — "Messy minds. Powerful results."
19. For people who [specific private behaviour].
20. Built for [tribe identity], not [label they reject].
21. You were never [label]. You were [reframe].
22. [Tribe] don't [cliché]. They [truth].
23. Finally, [permission they've been waiting for].
24. Made for the ones who [tell].
25. [Identity] without the [shadow emotion].

Templates are scaffolding. The winning line usually breaks the template once it has done its job.

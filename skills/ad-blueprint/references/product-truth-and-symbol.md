# Product truth and Symbol Lock

## Claim ledger (complete before writing copy)

- **User-provided product fact:** explicitly in the brief; may need checking for publication.
- **Verified fact:** source and exact substantiated proposition.
- **Audience inference:** creative hypothesis about feelings/behavior, not research.
- **Unverified objective claim:** numerical, comparative, certified, endorsed or guaranteed statement without proof. Do not use it in copy.

Avoid implied promises the product cannot deliver. Brand/legal requirements outrank design preferences. If evidence is missing, prefer expressive but non-quantified truth over invented facts.

## Symbol Lock (after selecting concept, before final render)

Record:
1. **Symbol:** one object, gesture or distinctive compositional device.
2. **Product link:** the specific, truthful product attribute it signifies.
3. **Instant reading:** likely cold-reader meaning and possible confusion.
4. **Distinctiveness:** what separates it from a cliché and from borrowed trade dress; this is a creative judgment, not a trademark clearance.
5. **Repeatability:** can it remain identifiable at billboard distance and on mobile?

**Evidence gate:** model 5D scores = subjective creative opinions; visually inspected files = artifact facts; recall/poll results = observed viewer evidence; reach/leads/sales = observed business evidence. Never mix these categories.
